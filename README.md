# cadence
All-in-one workflow for SMBs — estimate → e-sign → invoice → payment in a single tool, with an AI builder.

## The problem

Small and mid-sized businesses typically stitch together separate tools to get paid: one for estimates, another for e-signatures, another for invoicing, and another for payments. Every handoff means re-entering data, chasing status across systems, and paying for multiple subscriptions — costing time, money, and cash flow.

## What Cadence does

Cadence consolidates the full quote-to-cash workflow into one product:

- **Estimates** — create and send professional estimates
- **E-sign** — customers approve and sign without leaving the flow
- **Invoicing** — signed estimates convert into invoices with no re-entry
- **Payments** — customers pay directly from the invoice
- **AI builder** — generates and configures documents using AI, reducing setup time

## How it works

1. Create an estimate (manually or with the AI builder)
2. Send it to the customer for e-signature
3. On signing, Cadence converts it into an invoice
4. The customer pays online and the status updates automatically

## Product thinking

- **One source of truth** — data entered once flows through every stage, removing re-keying errors
- **Fewer tools, lower cost** — replaces several point solutions SMBs pay for separately
- **Faster time to cash** — fewer handoffs means less delay between agreeing a job and getting paid
